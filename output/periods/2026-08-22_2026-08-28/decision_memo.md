# Decision memo — FlashEats late deliveries

**For:** VP Operations · **cc:** Support Lead, Dispatch, Fleet Ops, Restaurant Ops, Product · **Run:** `run_weekly_2026-08-22_2026-08-28` · **Data gate:** WARN

## Situation

FlashEats promises an ETA at checkout. In August, **55.2%** of 375 validated deliveries missed it (median 7.6 min late; 22.13% were more than 10 min late). Leadership's "56%" is right for the dashboard definition (55.41%).

## Complication

- The delay is created **before pickup**, not on the road: 98.43% of overrun minutes on late orders accrue before the driver collects the food. Dispatch's own pickup estimate is short by a median 8.0 min.
- Late orders turn into support contacts 30.43% of the time (vs 7.74% on time); today's interventions reach 26.93% of orders with no visible effect on the late rate.
- The data cannot say whether the kitchen or the rider owns the pre-pickup delay: no *arrived at restaurant* event exists. GPS cannot fill the gap: before pickup, pings move AWAY from the restaurant in 99.0% of orders, and 91.0% of tracks are perfectly straight constant-speed lines, so the pings look interpolated rather than measured.
- The weather explanation does not hold up: the client's weather label agrees with observed rainfall only 54.1% of the time (kappa -0.004).

## Resolution — recommended actions
